How to read your request

Done
Stage finished. Shows the date and the work days it took.
Current stage
Where your request is now, with its due date.
Late
The stage has taken longer than its allowed work days.
Waiting
Starts when the stage before it is done.

Good to know

  • Work days are Sunday to Thursday. Fridays and Saturdays are not counted. Each stage's clock starts the day the previous stage is marked done.
  • OD BU updates every stage. OD BU marks each stage as done once it is completed, including OD Group, HRIS and HCM.
  • Comments. OD BU can add a comment on any request at any time — check your request for updates or questions.
  • Requisition number. After HCM, OD BU opens the requisition on Taleo and enters its number on the request, so you can follow up with recruitment.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel your own request only while it is still in the OD BU stage.
  • Plan ahead. Allow at least 20 work days between submitting the request and the date you need the person, plus the time recruitment needs to hire.
